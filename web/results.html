How Brainclip works

No clip button.
Just your brain.

  1. Wear the headset and playThe first minute is a warm-up, so Brainclip learns what normal looks like for you.
  2. Your brain spikesWhen excitement or focus jumps well above your last minute, that moment gets flagged.
  3. Get your reelBrainclip cuts those moments from the recording and shows you what your brain did in each one.
